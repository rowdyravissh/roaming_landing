Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Do I keep my regular phone number and WhatsApp? ▼

Yes! Your digital eSIM functions purely for high-speed data. Your primary SIM stays active for WhatsApp, iMessage, and 2-factor authentication codes from your bank.

When should I install the eSIM? ▼

You can install it before boarding your flight using your home Wi-Fi. The data validity period only begins once you land in your destination country and the eSIM connects to the local tower.

Can I share my connection (personal hotspot)? ▼

Yes, personal hotspot and data tethering are supported on all our travel plans, allowing you to connect your laptop or tablet seamlessly.
